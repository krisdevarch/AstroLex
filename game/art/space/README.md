# Space items

Act 1's lost everyday items (teacup, bread, spoon, key, desk lamp, door, envelope, chair, TV), orbit junk and astronaut items (satellite, solar panel, helmet, gears, wrench, battery, backpack, glove, camera, thermos, cables, a gear with a tag), asteroids, comets, a rocket and a sputnik. They are the decoys: each decoy tile is drawn as one of them and drifts with the letters (`game/scenes/tile_view.gd`, `ITEMS`).

Source: a sticker sheet (`docs/design/space-items-sheet.jpg`) the owner supplied in the project thread on 13 Oct 2026 and asked to use in place of the earlier planet sheet. Each object was cut out on transparency by flood-filling the white background (enclosed white gaps, such as inside a strap, were cleared too) and saved at its native size, about 60 to 150 px. Licence: the owner generated the sheets with an image tool and cleared them for use in the game (13 Oct 2026, project thread). The tool's terms for commercial release are the owner's to keep on file.
