# Babel

`babel.png` is Babel's sprite: the purple-suited astronaut with the yellow scowl, a cape and a letter gun. He flies in from the top right at the start of each level, says his line in a bubble and shoots the level's letters from the gun (`game/scenes/babel_view.gd`).

Source: the owner's character image (`docs/design/babel-character.jpg`), supplied in the project thread on 16 Oct 2026. Cut out on transparency by flood-filling the flat navy background from the edges (the dark cape lining and suit are inside the outline, so they stay), cropped to the figure and scaled to 462 × 547. The muzzle of the gun sits at about (0.93, 0.32) of the image.
