# WP-3.J: Bigger letter bubbles in liquid glass, lost items, painted backdrop and the game font

Owner feedback (13 Oct 2026, after WP-3.I merged): "can you make a letters a little bit more bigger", then, on colourful bubbles: "no just make the bubbles bigger, also i've noticed that the glass layout does not magnify the object behind. and it should look exactly like this" with a reference image of Apple-style liquid-glass controls (not committed: third-party art), "take the icon with the plus sign". Web only. Bubbles stay clear (no colour).

## Scope
- **Bigger bubbles and letters:** `tile.size` 0.13 → 0.15 and `glass.bubbleScale` 1.25 → 1.12, so a front bubble is 0.168 of the field width (was 0.1625; the owner trimmed the first 0.18 by about 5 px on a phone). The glyph is `tile.glyphScale` 0.75 of the tile (was 0.68 of 0.13). `tile.minGap` stays 0.03, so bubbles still never touch.
- **Room for them:** the spawner tries up to 256 spots and keeps the roomiest; after each refill and each tick, the separation runs a few passes so a crowded cluster settles.
- **Liquid glass like the reference "+" disc:** the bubble reads the live screen and acts as a convex lens: what is behind (planets, rockets, stars) shows magnified, `glass.magnify` 0.12 at the centre and more toward the rim (`glass.rimBend` 0.9), with a slight colour split at the edge. A thin bright rim, brightest top-left and bottom-right, a faint iridescent edge and the soft drop shadow; no highlight spot. `glass.live` 0 falls back to the baked sky. The square glass look is unchanged.

- **Lost items instead of planets (owner, 14:34: "change the items to match this", "remove the planets"):** the planets, sun and UFOs are gone. The floating objects come from the owner's second sheet (`docs/design/space-items-sheet.jpg`, cut out into `game/art/space/`): eight spots take Act 1's lost everyday items (teacup, bread, spoon, key, lamp, door, letter, chair, TV), three take orbit junk, and a comet, rocket or sputnik crosses now and then. Items are tinted toward the night sky as in mock v3, so the letters stay brightest.
- **Mock v3 (`docs/design/mock-v3.jpg`) and the owner's painted backdrop (`game/art/backdrop/low_orbit.jpg`):** the backdrop is scaled so its Earth's rim sits on the HUD curve. The HUD no longer draws its own Earth; `NightPanel` is now a veil over the painted city lights that lifts as words are restored. Top: the level's name in a centred chip (points under it) and "Level n / N" at right; the "AstroLex" chip is gone. Back-plane star alpha 0.85 → 0.6. After the owner's side-by-side check ("does not look nothing like this", "iterate until you match"), the bubbles follow the mock too: still a lens over the live screen, but darkened toward night navy inside (`glass.dark` 0.88), with an iridescent rim (`glass.film` 0.55) and a white highlight crescent and dot (`glass.gloss` 0.8). Items are muted by a shader (40 % of their colour, blue-grey tint); back-plane stars are pale and faint (alpha 0.15); chips are lighter pills with no border and larger text; the portrait ring is a dark ring outlined in the suit colour.
- **Font (owner: "for the whole game use Libre Caslon Condensed"):** `game/fonts/LibreCaslonCondensed.ttf` (OFL 1.1, `game/fonts/OFL.txt`) at weight 700, set as the default font at startup (`game/scenes/game_font.gd`). Setting it as the project's custom font failed CI: a fresh import loads that setting before the font is imported and logs a parse error.
- **Backdrop brightness fix:** the backdrop's saturation shader multiplied the texture by `COLOR`, which in Godot 4 already holds the texture, so the sky drew squared (about 3× too dark). It now uses `COLOR` alone; the item shader does the same.
