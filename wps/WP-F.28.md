# WP-F.28: Intro screen with the crew, and a crisp HUD

Owner request (29 Oct 2026, project thread): "polish and improve the resolution and layout of the game HUD elements. they are good but they look like they are in very bad resolution. also can you create a intro screen that will look similar to this one but with our characters?" The reference image is key art: a deep-blue starfield with a diagonal purple nebula band, a chrome "AstroLex" logo in the middle and four characters in glossy glass bubbles around it.

## What we found
The console tray, the three round buttons and the hint bulb are crops of the owner's 572x1024 JPEG mock (WP-F.12, `scripts/art/cut_hud.py`), upscaled 1.89x to game pixels. They carry the JPEG's blur and the upscale's soft edges, and the porthole shows the mock's scan-line noise. Their imports have no mipmaps, and the console is imported lossy, so a phone that draws the 1080-wide game smaller than 1080 device pixels also aliases them.

## Acceptance criteria
1. **Intro screen.** On launch the game opens on an intro screen before the title: the deep-blue starfield with a diagonal nebula band (top left to bottom right), a chrome "AstroLex" logo in the middle (dark outline, cyan glow, magenta underside) and the four Catchers in glass bubbles around it, placed as in the reference: Wren top, Juno large top right, Ash bottom left, Pip bottom right. The bubbles float in and bob out of step, the logo catches a gleam, and a "Tap to start" line pulses. A tap anywhere, or `intro.holdSec` seconds, goes to the title screen. Reduced motion holds everything still. It is skipped on autoplay, after a graphics-loss reload and with `?intro=0`, and shows once per launch (Back from the map goes to the title). Tests cover show, tap, timeout, the skips and the four Catchers.
2. **Intro art.** The sky and the logo are generated by `scripts/art/make_intro.py` (reproducible, no outside images); the bubble portraits are cut from the 1024 px Catcher art. Placeholder art until O-14 assets land.
3. **Crisp HUD.** The console tray, Pause, Sound and Settings buttons (with their icons) and the hint bulb are redrawn as vector art in the same shapes and places, rendered at 2x game pixels, imported lossless with mipmaps and drawn at half size. Every HUD position stays where it was (the `MOCK_*` layout), the porthole has no baked scan lines and the icons are sharp. Before and after crops of a 3x phone frame are in the evidence.
4. **Layout.** The right button column and the hint bulb share one centre line, and the HUD's outer margins match `SIDE_PAD` on both sides.
5. **Checks.** Schema entry for the new tunable; `validate_data`, `pytest tools` and `scripts/godot/test.sh` pass; the web export boots, and frames of the intro, the title and the play screen are in `reports/WP-F.28/`.

Out of scope: the start screen's own design, raising the 2x pixel-ratio cap (WP-F.16), final human-made art.
