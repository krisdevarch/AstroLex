# WP-F.21: Anchored drift

Owner request (26 Oct 2026, project thread "Build anchored drift letter layout"): the letter field is chaotic. Letters orbit, dart and overlap, so catching the right one is a fight; "people will quit playing". The owner picked the playful anchored-drift layout from the investigation page (`/mnt/project-files/design/letter-layout/letter-layout-options.html`, option 1) and asked for letters that "drift as smooth and polished as possible". Colour rings for needed letters are the next WP, not this one.

## The idea

Every catchable letter owns an **anchor**, one of a fixed set of points laid out on a honeycomb over the field, spaced so two letters on neighbouring anchors can never touch. The letter floats around its anchor, and the anchors themselves move as one body (a slow sway and a wave rolling through), so the field is never still but the player can always read where a letter will be. One letter at a time **hops** to an empty anchor after a visible wind-up. A catch **ripples** the neighbours, which bob outward on a spring and settle back. There is one catchable plane; the mid plane is retired. Orbit and dart are retired.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Anchors.** `round.gd` builds the anchor set from `anchors.spacing` (centre to centre, field units), a honeycomb filling the field between `field.topMargin` and `field.height` with rows offset by half a spacing; `anchors.spacing` ≥ `tile.size` + `tile.minGap` + 2 × `anchors.maxOffset` holds (assert in a test), so two anchored letters cannot overlap whatever their offsets. Every catchable tile (plane 0) holds exactly one anchor; the mid plane is no longer used (`spawner.frontShare` 1.0, mid-plane code may stay). Spawns take the free anchor that keeps the best spread (seeded); when none is free a needed letter still spawns: a plain decoy is dissolved first to make room. `_decoy_room` and `spawner.maxLettersOnScreen` are capped by free anchors. The back plane (decorative shards) is unchanged.
2. **Motion.** A tile's position is `anchor + body(t) + wave(t, anchor) + bob(t, tile) + spring offset`, all pure functions of round time and seed, and smooth at `sim.stepSec` (no jumps larger than `anchors.maxOffset` per step except hops and Babel moves). Body sway: `anchors.swayX`, `anchors.swayY` amplitudes, `anchors.swayHz`. Wave: `anchors.waveAmp`, `anchors.waveHz`, `anchors.waveLen` (phase by anchor x and y). Bob: `drift.bob`, `drift.bobHz`, per-tile phase from the tile id. Spring: ripples (`drift.ripple*`), drone shyness (`drift.shy*`) and gust push the spring velocity; `anchors.spring` and `anchors.damping` bring the offset back; the offset is clamped to `anchors.maxOffset`. `drift.speed` keeps its meaning as the level-tunable pace: it scales `swayHz`, `waveHz` and the hop rate, with 1× at `anchors.speedRef`. Orbit, dart, curve and surge code, tile fields and tunables are removed (schema too). `_separate_tiles` stays as a safety net and a test shows it never moves an anchored tile over 2,000 steps with every motion on.
3. **Hops.** Every `hop.every` s (± `hop.jitter`, seeded, first at `hop.firstAt`; 0 turns hops off) one real letter hops to a free anchor: `hop_tell{tile_id, to}` then after `hop.windSec` the letter flies for `hop.flySec` along the straight line (the view draws the arc) and `hop{tile_id, from, to}` fires on take-off, `hop_land{tile_id}` on landing. Never hops: the shot's target, the queued tile, the hinted tile, a shielded or landing tile, a tile a drone targets. `fire()` on a hopping tile is allowed: the shot aims at its destination and lasts at least the remaining flight, so a tapped hopper is still caught. Difficulty presets set `hop.every` (Easy slower, Hard faster); Act I levels before `hop.fromLevel` have no hops.
4. **Babel and drones.** Volley fills free anchors with decoys (capped by free anchors). Gust pushes the body offset (spring) in its direction over `babel.gustSec` and it springs back. Scramble is every eligible letter hopping at once to a seeded permutation of free and vacated anchors (one `babel_move{move:"scramble", moves}` event as today, over `babel.scrambleSec`). Shield unchanged. A letter Babel fires mid-round lands on its anchor (`landing` as today). A drone lifts a letter off its anchor; a letter dropped by a downed drone takes the nearest free anchor. The autoplay bot still wins the sample levels it wins today (`test_bot_still_wins_with_all_motion_on` and the Act II–IV move tests).
5. **View.** `tile_view.gd`/`field.gd`: a hop tell is a gold ring flash and a squash wind-up; the flight is an arc (`hop.arcPx` lift at mid-flight, view only) with a stretch along the motion; landing squashes and sends a short ring ripple from the landing point. Ripples from a catch are visible as the neighbours' spring bob (the rules move them). A miss (`escape`) makes the nearest letter flinch (small squash). The tether warms from the Catcher colour toward coral with the combo (`tether.comboWarm`). Reduced motion: no arc lift, no flinch, hops fade out and in at the new anchor, as scramble does today.
6. **Checks.** Every number is a tunable with a schema entry and a line in the evidence. `python -m astrolex_tools.validate_data`, `python -m astrolex_tools.levels`, `pytest tools -q`, `scripts/godot/test.sh` pass; the web export wins a round on autoplay in Chromium; screenshots of a hop tell, a hop in flight and a catch ripple in the evidence. `docs/PROJECT-BRIEF.md` and `wps/_index.md` updated.

Out of scope: colour rings for needed letters (next WP), per-act anchor shapes (ring, column), the web toy (backlog), new sounds beyond reusing the existing hop/landing style.
