# WP-F.18: Babel in the corner, drones from three sides, livelier letters

Owner request (25 Oct 2026, project thread): Babel always present in a circle in the top-left corner, shooting the letters the player must catch; drones come from any direction except the bottom. Then: "make the letters moving a bit more alive. After playing some levels it looks boring, like you do the same thing over and over again."

Design call agreed in the thread (mock: project files `design/babel-corner/`): Babel fires the opening volley all at once from the corner, as today's throw does, and then re-fires every letter that appears mid-round (refills, stolen letters coming back). He never drip-feeds the needed letters during the clock, so stars still measure the player. Act I keeps drones top-only; side entries start in Act II.

## Acceptance criteria
1. **Babel's corner.** During play Babel sits in a round porthole in the top-left corner (`babel.cornerX`, `babel.cornerY`, `babel.cornerSize`), mirroring the Catcher porthole, with idle life (bob, breathing) and no full-body fly-in. His lines appear in a speech bubble attached to the porthole, replacing the separate top band. Nothing in the top HUD overlaps the porthole or the bubble at 1080x1920 or on a 430x932 phone viewport.
2. **He shoots the letters.** The opening throw launches every letter from the porthole's muzzle (recoil and flash per shot, as today). Every letter that spawns mid-round (refill, a stolen letter coming back) flies out of the muzzle to its place instead of growing in. Reduced motion: letters fade in at their place, Babel does not recoil.
3. **Drones from three sides.** A drone enters from the top, the left or the right edge, never the bottom (`thief.sideShare`; side entries only in the upper `thief.sideMaxY` of the play area). A drone carrying a letter escapes to its nearest edge among left, right and top. Act I levels set `thief.sideShare` to 0. The drone view enters from the side it spawned on.
4. **Livelier letters.** Real letters move in three seeded styles: wander (today), orbit (`drift.orbit*`) and dart (`drift.dart*`, glide, ease off, then a short burst in a new direction, capped at `drift.dartBoost`). Letters edge away from nearby drones (`drift.shy*`) and are pushed outward by a catch (`drift.ripple*`). Each letter breathes (`tile.breathe*`) and stretches along fast motion (`tile.stretch`). All motion stays deterministic for a seed; reduced motion turns off breathing and stretch.
5. **Difficulty holds.** Mean letter speed over a round stays within 10 % of today's for the same tunables; the tap and tether tolerances still hit a darting letter (test); the autoplay bot still wins the Act I and Act II sample levels it wins today.
6. **Checks.** Every new number is a tunable with a schema entry. `python -m astrolex_tools.validate_data`, `python -m astrolex_tools.levels`, `pytest tools -q` and `scripts/godot/test.sh` pass. The web export boots and wins a round on autoplay in Chromium; screenshots of the corner, a side drone and a mid-round re-fire go in the evidence.

Out of scope: new level types or modifiers, scoring and progression changes (the engagement ideas are a separate write-up for the owner), Babel's art itself.
