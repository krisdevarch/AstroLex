# WP-F.12: Play screen to the target mock, cute HUD animations, quiet stars

Owner ask (19 Oct 2026, project thread, with `target-play-screen-mock.jpeg`): "match the game design to look as close as possible to this and add some very cute animations to each element that you think could be animated, and check one more time overall look on the whole game. Also remove the objects in the background, just use some star animation that won't clutter the screen so much" (decision O-31). Built on main after PR #57 (the owner's four act skies).

## Acceptance criteria
1. The play screen follows the target mock: the level name pill top left and "Level n / N" top right; Babel's speech bubble sized to his line with a tail curling down left; rainbow-rimmed glass letter bubbles; a console tray at the bottom with the Catcher in a round neon porthole and a slot screen (cream letter tiles, glowing empty slots, both words centred); a hint bulb above a column of round Pause, Sound and Settings buttons at the right; a coral liquid clock bar under the console. No name pill.
2. Each HUD element has a small idle animation, and every one rests under reduced motion: the pills bob and pop in on a new level, Babel's bubble pops in from its tail and bobs, the bulb glows, wobbles and hops, the porthole arcs drift and the Catcher breathes, the console lights blink, the button rings turn and buttons squish on press, the clock liquid sloshes with bubbles, empty slots breathe, and a landing letter squashes and springs.
3. The floating sticker objects are gone: the back plane is small soft stars that twinkle as they drift, the backdrop's sticker sparkles become tiny fading stars and a few soft glints, shooting stars are fainter and rarer, and the floating chair is painted out of the Low Orbit sky. The act paintings' planets, moon and nebula stay (they are the act's sky, not floating objects).
4. Whole-game look check: every screen toured in the web build; the title logo letters bob in a wave, the picked Catcher bobs in their porthole, the title footer is readable, and the Earth veil's vertical seams are gone.
5. All Godot tests pass with no SCRIPT ERROR; data validation passes; the web export boots.
