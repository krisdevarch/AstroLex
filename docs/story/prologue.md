# Prologue: wordless (decision O-28)

status: draft

Source: plan v3 §1.5 (Prologue row), Design Direction §3 (wordless Prologue), §8 (Hades: signature colours), §9 (colour story).
Rule: NO text in any still. No captions, no subtitles, no signs, no lips-synced speech bubbles. The only words in the whole sequence are the HUD slot letters and the word WATER, in play.
Palette (grey, until the first letter is caught): #000000 #0D0D0D #36454F #4B0082 #FFFFFF. Colour arrives only with the first caught letter.
Total runtime: 70 seconds (stills 40s, taught play about 30s, player-paced and capped). Skippable at any time after the first view.

## Stills

### S1. The call (7s)
- Shown: Earth at night, a small grey room. Tomas (about 20) at an open window, one hand cupped at his mouth, shoulders lifted as if shouting. A thin line of breath leaves him and nothing follows it: the air around his mouth is perfectly still. On the sill, a battered handheld radio, its light off.
- Palette: #0D0D0D room, #36454F window light, #FFFFFF breath, a single #4B0082 shadow.
- Sound: a rising breath, then the shout is cut: all audio drops to a dull room tone. The silence is the loudest thing.
- Emotion: helplessness, the strain of a voice that will not come.

### S2. The unnamed child (7s)
- Shown: a street, grey and still. A mother crouches in front of a small child, one finger pointing at the child's chest, then at her own mouth, then back. Her face is searching, not angry. The child tilts their head. Behind them, other figures stand in small pairs, mouths slightly open.
- Palette: #36454F figures, #0D0D0D street, #FFFFFF for the mother's pointing hand only.
- Sound: a single soft hum (the start of Tomas's leitmotif, three notes) that stops mid-phrase, as if the singer forgot the next note.
- Emotion: tender grief; love without a handle to hold it by.

### S3. The watcher (8s)
- Shown: the Scriptorium, a round window onto a dim grey Earth. Rhee, elderly, in silhouette with a pencil behind her ear, one palm flat against the glass. Her desk behind her: stacked handwritten cards, one lamp, off. She turns her head slightly toward the viewer, then back to the planet.
- Palette: #000000 void, #36454F planet, #FFFFFF a thin rim of light on her cheek, #4B0082 only in the deepest shadow of the room.
- Sound: low station hum, a slow pulse like a heartbeat; Rhee's leitmotif as a single held note on strings, unresolved.
- Emotion: vigil, weariness, a private weight (a hint, not an answer).

### S4. The first tile (6s)
- Shown: black space. A single glass letter tile drifts in from the left edge, turning slowly. It is the only thing with its own light: a small white glow from inside. It slides past the planet and stops near the viewer, hovering, faintly bobbing.
- Palette: #000000 sky, #FFFFFF tile glow, #36454F reflections on the glass.
- Sound: one clear glassy tone, then a quiet shimmer. The room tone falls away.
- Emotion: wonder; the first sign that something is still out there.

### Play beat: three-step teaching (about 30s, no text)
The Catcher's gloved hand and visor enter from the bottom of the frame. A ghost hand icon (no text) demonstrates each step. The target is the word WATER; its five slots appear on the visor edge as empty outlined boxes. Letters W, A, T, E, R drift in at easy speed. Each step repeats the ghost hand until the player does it, then fades.

1. **Tap (about 8s).** One tile (W) glows slightly brighter than the others. The ghost hand taps it; a soft ring pulses on the tile. The player taps it. Tile enlarges a little, a click tone. Teaches: tiles are things you touch.
2. **Tether (about 10s).** After the tap, a cyan line (#00DDFF) fires from the Catcher's glove and reels the tile in with an ease-out, landing in slot 1 with a flare. This is the first colour in the game. The ghost hand shows a short drag or tap-and-hold; the player does it for A. Sound: line zip, a soft pulse on the catch, the music gains one layer. The grey sky warms by one shade at the horizon.
3. **Finish a word (about 12s).** The ghost hand vanishes. The player is left with T, E, R and the empty slots; the right tiles glow gently brighter (light-as-hint). Each catch fills a slot, adds a music layer and lights more of the backdrop. No timer, no penalty. If a wrong tile is tapped it simply bobs away.

### S5. The first word (6s)
- Shown: all five slots lit white, spelling WATER. The word is the first readable text in the entire game. It rises off the visor into the frame, large, set in the glyph style the game uses (white, per Design Direction §9). The Earth behind it softens to blue.
- Palette: #FFFFFF word on #000000; behind it, the first hint of the Act I palette (#062C43, #054569).
- Sound: the full leitmotif resolves into a chord, the glass chime of the finished word, a breath of wind (the first environmental sound since S1).
- Emotion: relief, the feeling of a held breath released.

### S6. The coral window (5s)
- Shown: back to Tomas at his window. His lips shape one word, and a single small coral light comes on in a distant window of the city below. He is holding a glass of water, lifting it slowly. He is smiling, barely, surprised. Rhee, in a tiny inset glow at the screen edge, presses a hand to her mouth.
- Palette: #FF6B35 coral (Tomas), #FFFFFF glass, #36454F everything else.
- Sound: a trickle of water; the Tomas three-note hum, now complete.
- Emotion: hope; the cost and the point of the game in one gesture.
