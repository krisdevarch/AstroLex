# Story samples: Babel lines and chat comms

status: draft

Babel speaks only in letters the player has caught. For each line, the pool is every letter of the level's target words, with multiplicity. Tallies show pool vs used; used never exceeds pool. Punctuation and spaces are ignored.

Deliberately short (2 to 4 words) so every line fits by hand. Template shapes used: `{N} OR {N}?`, `WHO {V}?`, `SO {A}.`, `{A} {N}.`, `NO {W}.`, `{ANAGRAM_T}` with a gloss.

## B. Sample chat comms
Each exchange is under 60 words, skippable. "___" marks a word Tomas has lost.

### Rhee (amber)
1. (Act I, after the first level)
   - Rhee: Good catch. Your tether was a little eager. It will learn manners.
   - Rhee: I keep the cards you are restoring. Someone's handwriting is on them, and some days it is like reading my own.
2. (Act II, after a hard level)
   - Rhee: That was an old word. I did not expect it to come back so easily. Perhaps it forgave us.
   - Rhee: Get some air, Catcher. The work will still be here, which is both the comfort and the trouble.

### Ade (seafoam)
1. (Act I)
   - Ade: Kit says you caught a lamp before you caught a door. Priorities!
   - Ade: I told Kit a lamp is where you start. A lamp is a promise there will be a room.
2. (Act II)
   - Ade: Okay, the nebula is gorgeous and also I have lost my left glove in it. If you find a glove, it is a love story.
   - Ade: Do not tell Kit I said "love story."

### Kit (pink)
1. (Act I)
   - Kit: Ade is going to tell you he was right about the lamp. He was not. I have receipts.
   - Kit: You are doing great though. Not as great as me, but I started earlier.
2. (Act III)
   - Kit: This tower is giving me a headache. Every corridor has a door and every door says a different "no."
   - Kit: Stay close to the comms, yes? I am not saying I worry. I am saying I check.

### VANTA (crimson, Acts II-III only)
1. (Act II, first appearance)
   - VANTA: I cleared the nebula in half your time. Old records are a crutch.
   - VANTA: Tell Rhee her cards are charming. Charming does not catch fast.
2. (Act III, softening)
   - VANTA: I watched your run. You reeled that last tile like you meant it.
   - VANTA: Do not get used to this. But good catch.

### Tomas (coral)
1. (Act I, early, half-empty)
   - Tomas: Gran. I ___ at the window again. The ___ is dark and I keep trying to say ___ and nothing comes. Is anyone ___ there?
   - Tomas: I know your ___ is ___. I ___ you.
2. (Act I, later, fuller)
   - Tomas: Gran, I drank a glass of water today and said the word out loud. It came out all at once, like it never left.
   - Tomas: Mum cried. Then she laughed. Then she said my name. Thank you.
